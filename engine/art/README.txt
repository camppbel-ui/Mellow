Your own pictures for the sleep screen.

Drop .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp or .gif files straight into this folder (no
subfolders) and they appear under "Your pictures" in Sleep screen settings on
the dashboard. The filename becomes the caption: Cafe_Terrace-at-Night.jpg is
shown as "Cafe Terrace at Night".

Landscape pictures around 1920 pixels wide look best on a monitor. Portrait
ones are shown whole, over a soft blurred copy of themselves.
